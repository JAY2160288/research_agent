# Research Brief: 생성형 AI 활용이 대학원생의 연구 생산성과 연구 품질에 미치는 영향

> RQ — How does generative AI use affect graduate students' research productivity and research quality, and under what conditions do benefits or harms arise?

## 요약

생성형 AI가 대학원생의 연구 생산성과 품질에 미치는 영향에 대한 직접적이고 엄밀한 증거는 아직 매우 적습니다. 대학원생 대상 혼합방법 연구(전문 글쓰기 과제)가 생성형 AI와 교육의 효과를 비교했으며, 효과는 사용자와 사용 맥락에 따라 달라진다고 보고합니다. 그 밖의 대학원생 연구는 대부분 소규모 질적 연구로, 이점과 함께 윤리·무결성 우려를 제기합니다. 연구 품질 측면에서는 아이디어 생성과 데이터 식별에는 유용하나 문헌 종합과 참고문헌 정확성(환각)은 약하다는 결과가 있고, 사용자의 전문성과 지도·교육이 품질을 좌우하는 조건으로 나타납니다. 효율성 향상이 더 깊은 학습이나 독립적 학문 사고로 이어진다는 증거는 부족하며, 인지적 오프로딩과 AI 의존이 박사과정생의 독립성을 약화시킬 수 있다는 우려가 문헌에서 제기됩니다. 지도교수와의 관계 변화도 논의되지만 대부분 정성적·개념적 논의입니다. 따라서 실제 논문 진행, 출판 성과, 원고 품질을 객관적으로 측정하는 무작위·종단 연구가 필요합니다. 한계로는 초록 기반 평가, 비동료심사 자료 포함, 학부생 중심 증거, 국내 문헌 미검색이 있습니다."

## 1. 주제 재정의

- RQ: How does generative AI use affect graduate students' research productivity and research quality, and under what conditions do benefits or harms arise?
- 독립변수: generative AI use (ChatGPT, LLM tools), AI training/instruction
- 종속변수: research productivity (time, output), research/writing quality, critical thinking and scholarly independence, research integrity
- 대상: Master's and doctoral students
- 핵심 개념: generative AI, ChatGPT, graduate students, research productivity, research quality, academic writing, literature review, research integrity, cognitive offloading

## 2. 조사 계획

전략: Six searches (five OpenAlex, one arXiv) restricted mainly to 2023 onward, covering graduate-student GenAI use, writing quality experiments, LLM literature review accuracy, ethics/integrity/supervision, and cognitive offloading. The arXiv productivity query returned mostly irrelevant bibliometrics papers and was not used. Evidence is based on abstracts only; many 2026 items are low-citation or non-peer-reviewed (Zenodo, SSRN, OSF).

- **rq1** What is the effect of GenAI on graduate students' writing and research productivity?

- **rq2** How does GenAI affect the quality of research outputs (writing, ideas, evaluation)?

- **rq3** How accurate and reliable are LLMs for literature review tasks?

- **rq4** What are the ethical, integrity, and supervision issues of GenAI in graduate research?

- **rq5** Does GenAI use lead to cognitive offloading and weaker scholarly independence?

## 4. 종합

### 합의

- Studies across graduate and general higher-education samples portray GenAI as speeding up drafting and related tasks, but efficiency gains are not matched by evidence of deeper learning or independent scholarly thinking. [1, 6, 13] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.7)

- LLM output quality for research tasks is uneven: GenAI helps with ideation and data identification but is weaker on literature synthesis and reference accuracy, so hallucination is a recognized risk. [2, 5, 21] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

- Graduate-student GenAI use raises integrity and supervision concerns, though the evidence is mostly small qualitative studies. [3, 8, 24] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.3)

### 상충

- **Does GenAI-generated or GenAI-augmented text match human research quality?** (A 1편 vs B 2편)
  - A: An LLM-based review-generation method is reported to match or exceed manual review quality. [11]
  - B: Reviewers found AI-augmented writing lacked research details and reflective insights, and benchmarks emphasize hallucination risk in literature tasks. [4, 5]
  - 원인 가설: Differences in task (automated pipeline with retrieval versus unaided student writing), evaluation criteria (developer-validated metrics versus expert peer review), and domain expertise of the user.

### 조건부

- The quality benefit of GenAI appears to depend on user domain expertise, private data input, and instruction, rather than on tool access alone. [1, 2] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.5)

- Whether offloading harms thinking may depend on whether AI substitutes for or scaffolds the user's own cognition. [10, 25] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

### 커버리지 메모

Evidence on graduate-specific productivity and quality is thin: one mixed-methods study (professional writing tasks) and several small qualitative or low-quality sources. Quality of research outputs is addressed mainly by indirect evidence from LLM benchmarks and expert ratings, mostly in other populations. Integrity and cognitive-offloading topics are covered mostly by general student samples. No causal study of thesis or publication output was found.

## 5. Research Gap

### G1. No rigorous causal studies measure GenAI effects on real graduate research outputs

[1, 7, 15] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.3)

No rigorous causal studies measure GenAI effects on real graduate research outputs (thesis progress, publications, time-to-degree); existing work is small qualitative or task-based.

- 제안 RQ: Does structured GenAI training change graduate students' thesis progress and manuscript quality over one to two semesters?
- 방법: Randomized or staggered-rollout trial with blinded expert rating of manuscripts and time logs
- 데이터: Master's/doctoral cohorts across disciplines; manuscript ratings, time logs, supervisor assessments

### G2. Long-term effects on scholarly independence and critical thinking in graduate students are argued conceptually but not measured longitudinally.

[6, 25] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.5)

- 제안 RQ: Do dependent versus autonomous GenAI use patterns predict later independent research competence among doctoral students?
- 방법: Longitudinal panel survey with performance-based research-skill tasks
- 데이터: Doctoral students tracked over 1-2 years

### G3. Reliability of LLM literature-review support is benchmarked on models, but not in the hands of graduate students with differing expertise.

[2, 5] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

- 제안 RQ: How do graduate students' domain expertise and verification practices moderate errors and quality in GenAI-assisted literature reviews?
- 방법: Experimental task comparison with error auditing of citations and synthesis
- 데이터: Graduate students of varying expertise; audited reviews

### G4. Supervision and institutional policy effects on GenAI use are explored only qualitatively in few disciplines.

[8, 23, 24] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.3)

- 제안 RQ: How do supervisor guidance and institutional policies moderate GenAI use and research integrity outcomes among graduate students, including in Korea?
- 방법: Multilevel survey plus interviews
- 데이터: Graduate students and supervisors across institutions

## 7. 한계와 신뢰도

- Evaluation is based on abstracts only; several abstracts were truncated, so effect sizes were not available.

- Search was limited to six queries; the arXiv query returned mostly irrelevant results.

- Many 2026 items are low-citation or non-peer-reviewed repository records (Zenodo, SSRN, OSF); reliability scores reflect this.

- Few studies are graduate-specific; much evidence comes from undergraduates, general students, or model benchmarks, limiting transfer.

- Korean-language literature was not searched directly; only one Korean nursing study appeared.

- Productivity was rarely measured objectively; most evidence is perceptual.
