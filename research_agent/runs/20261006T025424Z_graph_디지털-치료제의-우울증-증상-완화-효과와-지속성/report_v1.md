# Research Brief: 디지털 치료제의 우울증 증상 완화 효과와 지속성

## 요약
402편의 후보 중 87편을 평가했으며, 주로 인터넷 CBT와 앱에 대한 메타분석, IPD 메타분석, 일부 RCT와 서술적 리뷰로 구성된다. 성인 우울증에서 인터넷 CBT는 주로 대기자·통상치료·무접근 대조군보다 치료 직후 작거나 중간 정도의 효과를 보였고(d=0.41, 준임상 우울 g=0.39), 단독 앱은 g=0.33(k=6)이었으며 청소년도 비슷한 크기(d=0.33)였다. 개선은 추적 시점에도 완만하게 줄어들 뿐 사라지지 않았으나(3-6개월 g=0.30, 12개월 g=0.27), 이 수치는 거의 준임상 우울 IPD 분석과 소규모 시험에서만 나와 진단된 우울증의 지속성은 사실상 확인되지 않았다. 1차 진료에서는 REEACT가 추가 이점을 찾지 못한 반면 스웨덴 시험은 통상치료 대비 이점을 보였는데, 이는 설계와 맥락의 차이이지 직접적 불일치는 아니다. 악화(deterioration)는 대조군보다 인터넷 중재에서 드물었지만(3.4% vs 7.6%), 치료 종료 후 악화 추적은 검색된 근거에서 찾지 못했다. 지도형이 비지도형보다 낫다는 근거는 우울증 특이적 직접 비교가 아닌 광범위한 정신건강 메타분석에 의존하며, 순응도와 효과의 연관성도 상관적이고 중도탈락이 크다(평균 31%). 핵심 공백은 12개월 이후의 지속성과 재발 시점, 주의 통제·가짜 대조 대비 특이 효과, 그리고 보조 vs 단독 사용·동반 불안 같은 조절변수이며, 일부 IPD 결과는 초록이 잘려 확인하지 못한 검색 한계이기도 하다.

## 1. 주제 재정의
- RQ: How effective are digital therapeutics at reducing depressive symptoms, and how long do these improvements persist after treatment?
- 독립변수: digital therapeutics (software-based therapeutic interventions for depression)
- 종속변수: depressive symptom severity (alleviation), persistence/durability of symptom improvement over time
- 대상: individuals with depression or depressive symptoms
- 핵심 개념: digital therapeutics, depressive symptoms, treatment effectiveness, durability of treatment effects, long-term follow-up, major depressive disorder, symptom reduction

## 2. 조사 계획
전략: Search 2010 to present, emphasizing systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and randomized controlled trials with adult or adolescent participants with depression or elevated depressive symptoms, plus regulatory-cleared prescription digital therapeutics studies. Exclude protocol-only papers, general wellness or prevention-only apps without depressive symptom outcomes, and non-digital telehealth that is purely video therapy. Expect rq2 (durability beyond 12 months) and rq6 (adverse effects and sham-controlled designs) to be the thinnest in the literature.

- **rq1** How effective are digital therapeutics at reducing depressive symptom severity in the short term compared with control conditions?  
  쿼리: digital therapeutics depression randomized controlled trial efficacy; internet-delivered cognitive behavioral therapy depressive symptoms meta-analysis; smartphone app intervention depression symptom reduction; prescription digital therapeutic major depressive disorder
- **rq2** How long do reductions in depressive symptoms persist after digital intervention ends, based on follow-up assessments?  
  쿼리: digital intervention depression long-term follow-up sustained effect; internet CBT depression durability of treatment effects; digital mental health depression 12-month follow-up outcomes; relapse after computerized CBT depression; individual participant data network meta-analysis guided unguided internet CBT depression follow-up; internet-based CBT depression pooled effect size 6 to 12 month follow-up; long-term outcomes randomized trial online cognitive behavioural therapy depression 2-year follow-up
- **rq3** How do effects and durability differ by intervention type, delivery format, and level of human support (guided vs. unguided)?  
  쿼리: guided versus unguided internet intervention depression effect; therapist support digital mental health intervention depression outcomes; app-based versus web-based depression intervention comparison; computerized CBT human support moderator effectiveness
- **rq4** How do patient characteristics and clinical context (severity, comorbidity, primary care vs. community, adjunct vs. standalone use) moderate outcomes?  
  쿼리: moderators of digital intervention outcomes depression baseline severity; digital therapeutics adjunct to antidepressant treatment depression; eHealth intervention depression primary care effectiveness; digital CBT comorbid anxiety depression subgroup analysis; individual participant data meta-analysis baseline severity moderator internet depression treatment; meta-regression clinical setting comorbidity digital CBT depression effect size; blended or adjunctive digital CBT versus standalone depression randomized trial
- **rq5** How do engagement, adherence, and attrition relate to symptom improvement and its persistence?  
  쿼리: adherence engagement digital depression intervention symptom improvement; dropout attrition internet-delivered depression treatment; dose-response app usage depressive symptoms; machine learning predict engagement mental health app
- **rq6** What methodological limitations, placebo or attention-control effects, and adverse effects affect the interpretation of digital therapeutic evidence for depression?  
  쿼리: risk of bias digital mental health depression trials; placebo sham control digital therapeutic depression; negative effects deterioration internet interventions depression; real-world effectiveness digital mental health depression

## 4. 종합
### 합의
- In adults with depression, internet CBT beat mostly waitlist, usual-care or no-access controls at post-treatment, with small-to-moderate effects (d=0.41; g=0.39 in subthreshold depression; d=0.66 for unguided CBT vs waitlist in one small trial; superior to TAU at 3 months in primary care). Standalone apps show g=0.33 (k=6), and a transdiagnostic app meta-analysis gives g=0.28 that stayed robust after adjusting for control type and risk of bias, but that one is indirect for clinical depression. [10.1080/16506070903318960, 10.1159/000507819, 10.1080/16506073.2011.616531, 10.1371/journal.pone.0028099, 10.1192/bjp.bp.114.160101, 10.1038/s41746-019-0188-8, 10.1002/wps.20673]
- Effects in youth (d=0.33 vs no intervention; g=0.45 for pooled depression and anxiety vs mostly waitlist) are similar in size. Estimates from the 2006 mixed anxiety/depression meta-analysis (d=0.27-0.32), the stress-management review (d=0.34, depression secondary) and the insomnia review (SMD=-0.42) are indirect for clinical depression. [10.3389/fpsyt.2019.00759, 10.1007/s10567-018-0271-8, 10.1017/s0033291706008944, 10.2196/jmir.5774, 10.1038/s41746-023-00800-3]
- Gains persisted at follow-up in the studies with numerical data: g=0.30 at 3-6 months and g=0.27 at 12 months in subthreshold depression, and lower depression than TAU at 6 months in helpline callers. This is a modest shrinkage, not a loss, but the numbers come mainly from one IPD analysis of subthreshold depression and one modest-sized trial. [10.1159/000507819, 10.1371/journal.pone.0028099, 10.1080/16506073.2011.616531]
- Reliable deterioration was less frequent with internet interventions than with control conditions in IPD meta-analyses (3.4% vs 7.6%; 5.8% vs 9.1%). A narrative review agrees, and an uncontrolled pre-post synthesis reports 2.9% deterioration, which is not comparable because it has no control. [10.1017/s0033291716001562, 10.1017/s0033291718000648, 10.1002/wps.20610, 10.2196/18100]
- Supported or guided formats tend to outperform unguided ones. Evidence comes from broad mental-health meta-analyses (SMD=-0.27 for symptom severity; d=0.61 vs 0.25), not depression-specific direct comparisons. Low-reliability narrative reviews support clinician support only as an engagement mechanism, and the stress guided advantage (d=0.64) is excluded as evidence for depression severity. [10.1016/j.invent.2014.08.003, 10.1080/16506070903318960, 10.1586/ern.12.63, 10.1007/s40501-023-00297-3]
- Higher engagement or adherence is associated with larger benefit (r=0.24; g=0.40 in broad mental health; larger depression effects in higher-adherence insomnia groups), but the link is correlational. Attrition is substantial (weighted mean dropout 31%, up to 50-83%). [10.3389/fdgth.2021.764079, 10.1038/s41746-023-00800-3, 10.1348/014466509x472138, 10.2196/jmir.1194]
### 상충
- **Whether digital CBT adds benefit in primary care (a design and context discrepancy, not a direct disagreement).**
  - A: Supported cCBT added to usual GP care gave no additional improvement over usual care at 4, 12 and 24 months. [10.1136/bmj.h5627]
  - B: Internet CBT beat treatment as usual in primary care at 3 months and in helpline callers at 6 months. [10.1192/bjp.bp.114.160101, 10.1371/journal.pone.0028099]
  - 가설: REEACT tested add-on use against unconstrained usual care in unblinded patients with PHQ-9 of 10 or more, while the others used standalone iCBT against TAU, with different programs, support and follow-up lengths. Any benefit may have been diluted by active usual care or low uptake.
- **Whether added human contact improves outcomes (a scope discrepancy).**
  - A: Lay telephone tracking added no benefit for symptoms or adherence. [10.1371/journal.pone.0028099]
  - B: Guided interventions outperformed unguided ones for symptom severity and completion across mental-health conditions. [10.1016/j.invent.2014.08.003]
  - 가설: Lay telephone tracking is a minimal form of support compared with the clinician or e-coach guidance in the pooled trials. The single helpline trial is small and the review is not depression-specific.
### 조건부
- Baseline severity moderates benefit: more severely depressed patients gained more from low-intensity interventions (guided self-help, internet) than from usual care. The effect interaction is small and the category is broader than digital-only. [10.1136/bmj.f540]
- Lower education raised deterioration risk, and male sex, lower education and comorbid anxiety predicted dropout from self-guided web interventions. [10.1017/s0033291716001562, 10.1017/s0033291715000665]
- Very large pre-post effects (pooled g=1.78 for depression) hold only as uncontrolled estimates for guided iCBT in routine care and are likely inflated, so they should not be compared with RCT effects. [10.2196/18100]

커버리지: The evidence is strong for short-term effects (rq1), with mostly small-to-moderate effects against waitlist or TAU controls, and for deterioration (rq6) from IPD meta-analyses. Durability (rq2) has numerical estimates only to 12 months in subthreshold depression. Results from the IPD network meta-analysis (6- and 12-month guided vs unguided) and the 2013 meta-analysis were not retrieved because of truncated abstracts, so that is a retrieval gap, with residual gaps beyond 12 months and in attention-controlled comparisons. Moderators (rq4), guidance effects specific to depression (rq3) and placebo or attention-control effects (rq6) are thin, with truncated results and population-specific or indirect reviews. Missing are IPD or meta-regression moderator results for comorbidity, setting and adjunct vs standalone use, long-term follow-up RCTs, and sham or attention-controlled trials.

## 5. Research Gap
- **Numerical durability estimates for clinical depression stop at 12 months, and even those come from subthreshold samples (g=0.27). The 6- and 12-month guided vs unguided estimates in the IPD network meta-analysis and the long-term results of the 2013 meta-analysis were not retrieved, so their numbers are unknown to us. Residual gaps are follow-up beyond 12 months, relapse timing, and how to keep a comparison once waitlist controls are treated.** [10.1159/000507819, 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2020.4364, 10.1186/1471-244x-13-113]
  - 제안 RQ: In adults with diagnosed major depression, how much of the post-treatment reduction in PHQ-9 from internet CBT remains at 12, 24 and 36 months, and when do relapses occur?
  - 방법: First extract the 6- and 12-month estimates from the existing IPD and meta-analyses, then run a pre-registered IPD meta-analysis of trials with at least 12-month follow-up, modelling PHQ-9 trajectories and time to relapse. Add a 24-36 month registry-linked follow-up of a completed iCBT trial cohort.
  - 데이터: IPD from the iCBT depression trials in the existing IPD consortia (13-42 trials) plus original trial authors' long-term data. Cohort follow-up could use IAPT or primary-care records and outcome questionnaires.
- **Most depression effects come from waitlist, TAU or no-access controls, and the placebo or attention component is unquantified. The only sham-like comparison is a 1-week chatbot trial with truncated results, and the transdiagnostic app estimate is not specific to clinical depression. Small waitlist trials may overstate specific effects.** [10.1002/wps.20673, 10.2196/40719, 10.1080/16506073.2011.616531]
  - 제안 RQ: How much of the effect of digital depression treatment over waitlist remains when it is compared with an attention-control or sham digital condition in clinically depressed adults?
  - 방법: Meta-regression of control type (waitlist, TAU, attention or sham) on depression effect sizes, restricted to clinical depression, plus a pre-registered three-arm RCT (iCBT vs attention-control app vs waitlist, 8-12 weeks). The primary outcome is PHQ-9 at post-treatment and at 6 months, with the attention-control arm offered iCBT after follow-up.
  - 데이터: Existing trial registries and the meta-analytic databases for coding control type. The RCT needs about 300 adults with PHQ-9 of 10 or more, recruited from primary care or online.
- **Moderators of symptom outcome are essentially untested in the retrieved results. Baseline severity shows only a small interaction with a broad 'low-intensity' category, while the REEACT trial found no add-on benefit and the Swedish trial found benefit over TAU, leaving adjunct vs standalone use, setting and comorbid anxiety unresolved. The two IPD meta-analyses' moderator results were not visible.** [10.1136/bmj.f540, 10.1136/bmj.h5627, 10.1192/bjp.bp.114.160101]
  - 제안 RQ: Do baseline severity, comorbid anxiety, clinical setting and use as adjunct vs standalone treatment moderate the effect of digital CBT on depressive symptoms?
  - 방법: Two-stage IPD meta-analysis with pre-specified treatment-by-moderator interaction terms on post-treatment PHQ-9, complemented by a pragmatic RCT in primary care randomising adjunct to usual care vs standalone iCBT vs usual care. Compare outcomes at 3 and 12 months.
  - 데이터: IPD from the consortia behind the JAMA Psychiatry and BMJ IPD analyses, with harmonised baseline variables. The RCT needs about 450 primary-care patients with PHQ-9 of 10 or more across several practices.
- **The advantage of guided over unguided formats comes from broad mental-health meta-analyses, not from depression-specific head-to-head comparisons. The only minimal-support test, lay telephone tracking, was small and found nothing. Whether the guidance advantage lasts beyond 12 months or can be replaced by cheaper support is unclear.** [10.1016/j.invent.2014.08.003, 10.1371/journal.pone.0028099, 10.1080/16506073.2011.616531]
  - 제안 RQ: Among adults with depression, does clinician-guided iCBT produce larger and longer-lasting PHQ-9 reductions than iCBT with automated or lay support, and by how much?
  - 방법: Three-arm non-inferiority RCT comparing clinician-guided, automated-feedback/lay-supported and unguided iCBT, with PHQ-9 at post-treatment, 12 and 24 months. Secondary outcomes are module completion and cost per remission.
  - 데이터: About 600 adults with major depression recruited through online screening or IAPT-type services, with pre-specified non-inferiority margin and sample-size calculations.
- **Deterioration is quantified only from baseline to post-treatment, and engagement is linked to benefit only correlationally. Lower education predicts both deterioration and dropout, but no study has followed deterioration after treatment ends or tested whether adapting to low-education users changes it. The causal role of engagement therefore remains untested.** [10.1017/s0033291716001562, 10.1017/s0033291718000648, 10.3389/fdgth.2021.764079]
  - 제안 RQ: Does an engagement-adaptive version of iCBT reduce deterioration and dropout among lower-education adults compared with standard iCBT, including during 12 months of follow-up?
  - 방법: Two-arm RCT with an adaptive version (simplified content, scheduled prompts) vs standard iCBT, stratified by education. The primary outcome is reliable deterioration on PHQ-9 at post-treatment and 12 months, with engagement mediation analysis.
  - 데이터: About 500 adults with depressive symptoms, oversampling those with primary or secondary education only. Recruit through primary care and community outreach.

## 7. 한계와 신뢰도
- Only abstracts and metadata were read, with no full texts; several key results (the IPD network meta-analysis 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2020.4364 with 6- and 12-month guided vs unguided estimates, the 2013 meta-analysis, the moderator results of the two IPD analyses, and the adherence review 10.2196/43727) were truncated, so these gaps are partly retrieval failures rather than true research gaps.
- Persistence evidence (g=0.30 at 3-6 months, g=0.27 at 12 months) comes from subthreshold depression and distressed helpline callers, not diagnosed major depression; REEACT's null result at 12 and 24 months argues against generalizing, and no numerical follow-up beyond 12 months was found.

- Guidance, engagement and intervention-type evidence is indirect: guided vs unguided contrasts come from cross-trial comparisons in broad mental-health or non-depression reviews (stress, insomnia, transdiagnostic apps), engagement–outcome links are correlational, and effects are not comparable across web iCBT, apps, chatbots and blended formats.
