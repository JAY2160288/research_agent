# Research Brief: 생성형 AI 활용이 대학원생의 연구 생산성과 연구 품질에 미치는 영향

> RQ — How does the use of generative AI affect graduate students' research productivity and research quality?

## 요약

524편의 후보 중 98편을 평가했으며, 근거는 설문, 소규모 질적 연구, 리뷰·논평, LLM 출력 검증 연구, 그리고 EFL 환경의 소규모 무작위 대조시험(RCT) 2편으로 이루어져 있다. 합의된 부분은 학생들이 주로 글쓰기·브레인스토밍·정보 검색·paraphrasing에 GenAI를 쓰고, 대학원생도 연구·논문 작성에 유용하다고 인식한다는 점이다. 다만 이 인식은 소규모 자기보고에 기반하며, 작성 속도·소요 시간·출판 성과를 실제로 측정한 연구는 하나도 없어 연구 생산성 주장은 검증되지 않은 상태다. 품질 측면에서는 LLM이 생성한 참고문헌에 허위·부정확 사례가 많고 DOI 정확도가 특히 낮다는 결과가 있으나, 정량 근거는 GPT-3.5와 10개 주제를 다룬 연구 1편에 의존한다. AI 보강 텍스트는 가독성은 높게 평가되지만 연구 세부와 성찰적 통찰은 부족하다는 잠정적 시사점이 있다. 상충 지점으로, AI 산출물에 대한 비판적 평가가 도움이 되는지 해가 되는지는 결과가 서로 대립한다기보다 측정 구성개념(지각된 품질·자기효능감 대 글쓰기 성찰)과 표본(일반 학부생 대 대학원·박사과정)이 달라 직접 비교가 어렵다. 가장 중요한 Gap은 대학원생의 생산성과 전문가 평가 기반 연구 품질을 통제 연구나 로그 기반으로 측정한 근거가 없다는 점과, 코딩·통계·데이터 분석 활용에 대한 근거가 전무하다는 점이다.

## 2. 조사 계획

전략: Focus on 2022 to present, since ChatGPT-era generative AI is the relevant intervention, and prefer empirical studies (experiments, quasi-experiments, surveys, interviews, mixed methods) plus systematic reviews on graduate or doctoral students. Exclude work limited to undergraduates or K-12, purely technical LLM benchmarks with no human users, and opinion pieces without data unless they supply policy context. Expect rq6 and the causal evidence on research quality in rq2 to be the thinnest, since most studies rely on self-reports rather than objective output measures.}

- **rq1** How does generative AI use affect graduate students' research productivity, such as writing speed, publication output, and time efficiency?

- **rq2** How does generative AI use affect the quality of graduate students' research, including writing quality, argumentation, methodological rigor, and originality?

- **rq3** For which research tasks and stages (literature review, data analysis, coding, writing, editing) do graduate students use generative AI, and how prevalent and varied is that use?

- **rq4** What mechanisms and moderators (AI literacy, discipline, prompting skill, supervisor guidance, institutional policy, English proficiency) shape the effects of generative AI on research outcomes?

- **rq5** What risks and unintended effects arise from generative AI use in graduate research, such as hallucinated citations, academic integrity violations, overreliance, and skill erosion?

- **rq6** How are research productivity and research quality measured in studies of generative AI in graduate education, and what do the designs and evidence quality look like?

## 4. 종합

### 합의

- Students report using GenAI mainly for writing and brainstorming support, information retrieval, paraphrasing and concept explanation, but the prevalence figures come from general or mixed student populations rather than graduate-specific samples. [37, 38, 39] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 4.0)

- Graduate and postgraduate students perceive GenAI as useful for research and thesis writing, including faster progress, but these are self-reported perceptions from small samples and none of these studies measures time saved, writing speed or publication output. [1, 4, 5, 9] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

- LLM-generated references are unreliable: a substantial share are fabricated or inaccurate, and DOI accuracy is especially poor. These tests used LLM outputs, not graduate-student samples, and mostly older models. [18, 40, 54] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.7)

- Surveys, reviews and commentaries raise concerns about accuracy, hallucination, integrity and overreliance, and recommend keeping human judgment central; this rests largely on low-reliability narrative reviews and perception data. [10, 23, 24, 37, 42] (근거 5편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.4)

- Cautiously, AI-augmented text may read better yet lack research depth: reviewers rated it higher on readability and informativeness but found it short on research detail and reflective insight, and AI reviews were rated slightly below peer reviews. The samples are small (17 HCI reviewers; an unspecified graduate course) and not specific to graduate research writing. [20, 21] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

### 상충

- **Whether critical appraisal of AI output helps or hurts outcomes** (A 1편 vs B 3편)
  - A: Critical appraisal of AI had a significant negative effect on AI self-efficacy and on AI output quality among university students. [17]
  - B: Qualitative studies of graduate students describe critical evaluation and feedback-seeking as central to responsible, beneficial use and to better writing. [1, 5, 7]
  - 원인 가설: The survey measures perceived output quality and self-efficacy, which may fall when students scrutinize outputs, whereas the qualitative work concerns rated or reflected writing quality and responsible use. The samples also differ: general Austrian university students in a cross-sectional SEM versus graduate or doctoral students reflecting on research writing.

- **Whether benefits of ChatGPT in academic writing outweigh the challenges** (A 1편 vs B 1편)
  - A: Research students with sustained ChatGPT use reported accelerated progress, higher quality and greater confidence. [5]
  - B: Postgraduate English-major students reported that the challenges, including overreliance and writing difficulties, outweigh the benefits. [10]
  - 원인 가설: The first group had at least four months of research use and were self-selected as experienced users, while the second were English majors writing in their own discipline, where language-quality gains matter less. Both rely on small self-reported samples, and the second has lower reliability.

### 조건부

- Better writing performance with GenAI is associated with iterative, highly interactive, collaborative use, and with high AI literacy and critical engagement; these are small, single-task studies and association rather than causation. [2, 3, 7, 8] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

- Randomized trials show writing gains from AI feedback or ChatGPT-assisted instruction only for EFL learners in structured instruction, and one used a Grammarly-based tool; the outcome is writing proficiency, not research output. [6, 19] (근거 2편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

- LLMs are said to lower the barrier to English academic writing for non-native researchers when used for editing and refining with disclosure, not for generating large portions of text; this is a commentary without empirical data. [42] (근거 1편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.0)

- Citation accuracy of ChatGPT depends on discipline: DOI accuracy was much lower in the humanities (8.5%) than in the natural sciences (32.7%), though only GPT-3.5 on 10 topics was tested. [54] (근거 1편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

### 커버리지 메모

rq1 (productivity) is effectively unanswered: no paper in the table measures writing speed, time-on-task or publication output, and the evidence is self-reported perceptions plus one n=1 undergraduate autoethnography, so productivity claims should be treated as unverified. rq3 prevalence rests on large but general-student surveys, with graduate-specific evidence limited to small qualitative studies, and no paper addresses coding, statistical analysis or data-analysis use, so that gap reflects absence from the table rather than evidence of non-use. rq2 and rq4 have moderate but small-sample evidence, mostly qualitative or single-task behavioral studies, with two small RCTs in EFL settings. rq5 is strongest for citation hallucination, via LLM-output tests not involving students, and thinner for integrity violations and skill erosion. For rq6, measurement is mostly perception or single-task rated writing. Missing are controlled or log-based studies of graduate students that measure time, output volume, publications and expert-rated quality over time, and surveys or experiments on coding and data analysis.

## 5. Research Gap

### G1. No study in the table measures graduate students' research productivity, such as time-on-task, writing speed, output volume or publication counts.

[4, 5, 9, 11] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 2.8)

The productivity claims rest on self-reported perceptions from small qualitative or survey samples. The only documented usage trace is an n=1 undergraduate autoethnography, which cannot support a graduate-level claim.

- 제안 RQ: Does access to a general-purpose generative AI assistant change graduate students' time-on-task and manuscript or thesis-chapter output compared with no access?
- 방법: Pre-registered RCT with 2 arms (GenAI access vs. no access) over one semester, with logged writing time and words of draft per week as primary outcomes and chapter or manuscript submission as secondary outcomes. Add keystroke and chat logging to separate productive use from idle use.
- 데이터: About 120 master's and doctoral students recruited through 2-3 universities' graduate schools, with writing-tool logs and advisor-confirmed milestone records. Stratify by discipline and English-language status.

### G2. Research quality is measured only through perceptions or single-task rated writing, never through expert…

[2, 5, 20, 21] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.0)

Research quality is measured only through perceptions or single-task rated writing, never through expert judgment of the research itself (originality, rigor, argument depth). Evidence suggests AI-augmented text reads better but lacks research detail and reflective insight, while graduate students perceive higher quality. These two signals have not been tested against each other with blinded expert ratings of graduate work.

- 제안 RQ: Do blinded expert raters judge graduate research drafts written with GenAI as higher in rigor, originality and argument depth than unassisted drafts, and does this match students' own quality ratings?
- 방법: Randomized crossover design where each student writes two comparable literature-review or methods sections, one with GenAI and one without, and blinded raters score them on a validated rubric. Compare expert scores with student self-ratings to estimate the perception-reality gap.
- 데이터: About 60 graduate students and 6-10 faculty raters from a research-methods course or writing program, with rubric scores and chat logs.

### G3. The evidence conflicts on whether critical appraisal of AI output helps or hurts.

[1, 2, 7, 17] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.2)

A general-student survey finds it lowers self-efficacy and perceived output quality, while graduate and doctoral qualitative studies present critical evaluation as central to good writing. The conflict is unresolved because the survey is cross-sectional with perceived outcomes and the qualitative studies have no objective quality comparison.

- 제안 RQ: Does training graduate students in critical appraisal of AI output improve expert-rated writing quality while lowering or preserving their AI self-efficacy?
- 방법: Two-arm randomized experiment comparing a critical-appraisal and verification training module with a basic tool-use module before a research-writing task. Primary outcome is blinded expert-rated quality, with self-efficacy and perceived output quality as secondary outcomes to test whether they diverge.
- 데이터: About 100 graduate students from multiple disciplines, recruited via graduate schools, with pre/post self-efficacy scales and rated writing samples.

### G4. Citation fabrication is demonstrated only in LLM-output tests with older models

[4, 18, 40, 54] (근거 4편 · 신뢰도 평균 3.5)

Citation fabrication is demonstrated only in LLM-output tests with older models (GPT-3.5, Bard) or narrow topics, never in what graduate students actually submit. It is unknown whether students catch fabricated references, whether discipline-dependent error rates carry over to current models, or whether fabrication affects thesis or manuscript quality.

- 제안 RQ: What proportion of fabricated or inaccurate references in graduate students' GenAI-assisted literature reviews survive student verification, and how does this vary by discipline and model version?
- 방법: Audit study in which students draft literature reviews with a current LLM, and all citations are verified against databases before and after student checking. Compare error survival rates across disciplines and between students with and without verification training.
- 데이터: About 80 graduate students across humanities, social science and natural science programs, with drafts, chat logs and bibliographic databases such as Crossref and Scopus.

### G5. Graduate-specific prevalence and task profiles are unknown because large surveys sample general or mixed student populations.

[37, 38, 39] (근거 3편 · 신뢰도 평균 4.0)

No study in the table covers graduate students' use of LLMs for coding, statistical analysis or data analysis, so the data-analysis side of research productivity and quality is unexamined.

- 제안 RQ: What share of master's and doctoral students use GenAI for coding, statistical analysis and data interpretation, and how is that use associated with analysis errors and perceived reliance?
- 방법: Stratified cross-sectional survey of graduate students, with a follow-up task in which a subsample completes a standard analysis with and without GenAI and errors are scored against a reference solution. Compare use patterns across disciplines and career stages.
- 데이터: A survey of about 1,000 graduate students across several universities or national doctoral networks, plus about 60 students for the analysis task.

## 7. 한계와 신뢰도

- Only abstracts and metadata were read, with no full texts, so details such as effect sizes, interaction-pattern results and numeric fabrication rates could not be verified; several abstracts (e.g., [18], [40]) give no extractable numbers.

- The search covered only OpenAlex and arXiv within a limited time window, and 98 of 524 candidates were evaluated, so relevant work in other databases or non-indexed venues may be missing.

- rq1 (productivity) is effectively unanswered: no paper measures time-on-task, writing speed or publication output, and the only signals are self-reports plus an n=1 undergraduate autoethnography; this was flagged as a major quality-gate finding after two replans and remained unresolved.

- Much of the evidence is low-reliability (narrative reviews, commentaries, perception surveys) and drawn from general or mixed student populations or EFL settings rather than graduate-specific research samples; the two RCTs measure writing proficiency, not research output.

- Evidence on integrity violations, overreliance and skill erosion (rq5) is thin and graduate-specific data are absent, and no paper covers coding, statistical analysis or literature-search workflows, so that gap reflects absence from the table rather than evidence of non-use.

- Some synthesis claims are weakly grounded: the AI-literacy-to-performance link and the critical-appraisal conflict compare non-comparable constructs, citation-fabrication rates rest on one GPT-3.5 study, and the AI-versus-peer review finding is peripheral to graduate students' own research quality.
